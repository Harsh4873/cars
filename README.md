# Cars

A swipe deck of used cars in the $5,000 to $8,000 range, published at `https://harsh.bet/cars/`.

The shortlist is Toyota and Honda ads checked on September 30, 2026, within a day trip of College Station. Each card has the asking price, miles, ownership, title notes, the seller's claims, and the known weak spots for that model. Passing and matching stay in this browser.
