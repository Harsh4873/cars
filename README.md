# Cars

A swipe deck of used cars in the $5,000 to $8,000 range, published at `https://harsh.bet/cars/`.

The deck is dealer listings from CarGurus and Allen Honda, checked on September 30, 2026. Every car is about 30 minutes from Texas A&M, about 30 minutes from Seven Lakes, or in Houston. Each card has the asking price, miles, ownership, title notes, the seller's claims, and the known weak spots for that model. Passing and matching save in this browser immediately. Approved owners can sign in with Google to sync history across devices through Firestore; see [Firebase setup](FIREBASE_SETUP.md).
